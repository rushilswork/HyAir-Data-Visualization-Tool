About HyAir

HyAir is an open, no-account air quality explorer for Indian cities. It started as a Hyderabad map and grew into a small analytics tool: live readings, rankings, pollutant comparison, a 72-hour forecast and plain-language health guidance.

Where the data comes from

Readings come from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API, which serves the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) model. These are modelled values on a grid of roughly 11 km, not readings from a single ground sensor, so they can differ from a monitor on your street.

About the numbers

  • US AQI is provided by the API.
  • India NAQI is computed in the browser from the pollutant concentrations using the CPCB breakpoints. The official index uses 24-hour averages for PM and 8-hour for ozone and CO; HyAir uses the latest hourly value, so treat it as an estimate.
  • Pollutant colours compare each value with the WHO 2021 guideline levels.

Features

  • Click anywhere on the map for the air quality at that spot, or use My location (the position never leaves your browser except as coordinates sent to Open-Meteo).
  • Upload your own data (lat,lon,aqi,pm2.5,pm10,so2,o3,co,no2,city); see sample-upload.csv.
  • Export the table as CSV, or copy a link to the exact view you are looking at.

This is an informational project, not a medical or regulatory source.
